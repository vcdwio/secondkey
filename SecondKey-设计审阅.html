What I left alone

Three things I flagged and then decided against touching.

  • The palette and the typeCream, deep green-black, copper accent, serif display. It works, and it is the one part of the interface a judge will recognise as deliberate. Changing it a week out buys nothing.
  • The empty space inside the quality-gate cardFilling it means inventing content. An honest gap beats a decorative one.
  • The 898-line control-room componentWorth splitting, but not before a deadline. It is a maintenance concern, not something a judge sees.

One thing worth knowing before you spend more on polish

There are two $5,000 design prizes, and they are not the same prize.

Best Multimodal UX rewards multiple input modalities — voice, vision, mixed media. SecondKey is text and screen. Honestly, it does not qualify, and more CSS will not change that.

Best Architectural Design rewards system architecture: the deterministic core, the split between what the model may draft and what it may never decide, the reversible/irreversible boundary expressed as a framework primitive. That is the strongest thing you have built, and it is won in the architecture diagram and the answers you give — not in pixels.
