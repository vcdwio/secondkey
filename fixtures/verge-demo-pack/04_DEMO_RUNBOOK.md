# Eight-Minute Demo Runbook

## 1. Establish the problem — 45 seconds

Verge Consulting has one team, seven active clients and limited capacity. On Monday morning, two client commitments become P0 at the same time.

## 2. Show incoming evidence — 75 seconds

Open BlueHarbor EM-001, Elevate EM-005, Morrow EM-008 and the internal accelerator EM-019. Emphasise that no single email contains the full answer.

## 3. Show Context Quality — 90 seconds

Demonstrate three traps: EM-004 is an obsolete BlueHarbor schedule; EM-025 duplicates EM-001; EM-030 comes from the wrong Alex Morgan. Then show that MT-BH-01 is the governing record.

## 4. Show the decision — 90 seconds

Open the flagship expected output. Explain why BlueHarbor and Elevate are P0, Morrow is P1 rather than P0, Ledgerwise is P2, and internal product work moves first.

## 5. Show resource allocation — 75 seconds

Explain the skill match and the downstream cost: Marcus and Noah leave the accelerator, Hannah gives two hours to Elevate, and Daniel retains two hours to capture reusable learning.

## 6. Show human approval — 60 seconds

Open the proposed outputs. Client emails, contractor spend, calendar moves and the CRM status remain drafts until Olivia Mercer approves.

## 7. Show audit and eval — 45 seconds

Open the decision trace and the Eval list. Finish with external_write=false and the ability to block release when expected evidence, permissions or priorities fail.

## Closing line

ContextOps does not simply produce an answer. It shows which evidence changed the decision, what work moves, who is affected, and what a human must approve.
