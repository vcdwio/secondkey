# Verge Consulting

Verge Consulting is a fictional Sydney-based operations, growth and AI transformation consultancy. It is the sole platform tenant in this demo. The seven companies in the pack are separate client accounts, not separate tenants or products.

## Operating model

Verge helps clients improve customer operations, sales processes, service delivery, research, reporting, training and AI-enabled workflows. The General Manager approves material resource and commercial decisions. The Product & Solutions Manager converts repeated consulting patterns into reusable service products. Account Managers own client commitments; Delivery manages capacity; consultants and analysts produce the work.

## Demo promise

One team. Seven clients. Limited capacity. ContextOps decides what matters, who should handle it, what trade-off is created, and what requires approval.
