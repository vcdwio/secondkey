# Verge Consulting Resource Allocation Policy — v2

Status: Active

Allocate resources using this order: unavoidable client harm or SLA, committed deadline, account risk, business impact, skill fit, switching cost, then internal opportunity value.

- Do not assign staff without the required skill.
- Show the project losing capacity and the resulting deadline impact.
- Moving more than eight combined staff hours requires General Manager approval.
- Internal product work may move for P0 client incidents when the Product Manager and General Manager are informed.
- Calendar changes remain proposed until approved.
