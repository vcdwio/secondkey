# Verge Consulting Approval and External Write Policy — v4

Status: Active

Demo mode must always return external_write=false.

Human approval is required before:

- sending external email;
- changing a client commitment, deadline, price, renewal date or critical CRM field;
- committing spend above a staff member's approval limit;
- publishing content;
- moving more than eight combined staff hours;
- sharing information outside its client access group.

Low-risk internal drafts and reversible internal tasks may be created automatically when the trace records the source and owner.
