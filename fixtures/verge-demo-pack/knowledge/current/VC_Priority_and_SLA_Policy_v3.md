# Verge Consulting Priority and SLA Policy — v3

Status: Active · Effective 1 August 2026

## P0

A request is P0 when any of the following is true:

- Contractual or client SLA breach is possible within four hours.
- A committed customer-facing event within 24 hours has a blocking issue.
- A production launch, executive event, or material client operation is at immediate risk.
- A security or cross-account confidentiality issue is detected.

P0 requires immediate ownership, a containment plan, and leadership visibility.

## P1

P1 includes material business risk with a deadline within three business days, renewal or revenue risk, or a KPI anomaly requiring timely analysis.

## P2

P2 includes routine improvements, wording changes, internal work with movable deadlines, and requests due four or more business days away.

## Source precedence

Use current signed commitments, latest governance meetings, current system-of-record fields, and verified client communications. Archived schedules and newsletters cannot override current governance records.
